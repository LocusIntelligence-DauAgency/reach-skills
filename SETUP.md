# Reach setup

Connect https://reach.locus-intelligence.com/api/mcp using Streamable HTTP. Sign in with your own Reach account through the client OAuth browser flow; no API key is needed. Connect your Google Business Profile in Reach for connected business data and profile management. Availability depends on account features and connection state. Never share credentials or tokens.

Choose the location before using a skill. Read tools first. Approve the exact public change before posting a reply, publishing or scheduling a post, or updating a profile.
